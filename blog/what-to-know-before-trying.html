What YU SLEEP Actually Contains

YU SLEEP lists 8 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Magnesium — A well-known mineral that supports muscle relaxation and a calmer nervous system, helping the body shift out of a wired, alert state at bedtime.
  • L-Theanine — An amino acid found naturally in tea that promotes a relaxed but clear-headed feeling, easing the mental chatter that often keeps people awake.
  • Melatonin — A hormone your body already produces to signal nighttime, included to help reinforce the natural sleep-wake cycle and make falling asleep feel easier.
  • Valerian Root — A long-used herbal ingredient associated with quieter evenings and a more settled transition into sleep.
  • Chamomile — A gentle flower extract traditionally taken in the evening to encourage a calm, unwound state before bed.
  • Passionflower — A botanical frequently included in sleep blends for its calming properties and its support for a more peaceful wind-down.
  • Lemon Balm — A soothing herb in the mint family that pairs with other botanicals to help take the edge off a busy day.
  • GABA — A naturally occurring brain chemical associated with a quieter, less overstimulated state that makes sleep easier to reach.

How It Is Meant To Work

YU SLEEP approaches sleep from two directions at once: quieting an overactive mind with amino acids and GABA while relaxing the body with magnesium and traditional calming botanicals.

Calms the overactive mind: L-theanine and GABA work to quiet the mental chatter and overstimulation that keep so many people awake long after the lights go out.

Relaxes the body: Magnesium helps loosen physical tension, letting your muscles and nervous system shift out of the alert state that blocks sleep.

Reinforces your sleep rhythm: Melatonin signals to your body that it is nighttime, supporting the natural sleep-wake cycle so falling asleep feels more automatic.

Layers in traditional botanicals: Valerian root, chamomile, passionflower, and lemon balm round out the formula with calming herbs that have been used for evening relaxation for generations.
